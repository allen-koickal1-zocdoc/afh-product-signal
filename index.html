How these numbers are made, and where they are weak
Read this before quoting anything below the top theme.
Grouping Every ticket is matched against an ordered list of regex patterns, first match wins, so each ticket lands in exactly one theme and the counts add up. Order is by root cause, not symptom: a patient who was billed out-of-network after a cancellation is counted under insurance, not cancellations.
Grouping accuracy Not measured on this run: the hand-labelled sets are held outside the repo and were not available. Treat every theme count as unvalidated.
Impact score 3× booking-blocking + 1× degrading + 0× cosmetic. 75% of the blocking count (79 of 105) is inferred from the theme, not read from the ticket: five themes are treated as blocking by definition, on the reasoning that a patient whose booking never reached the practice lost the appointment whether or not they said so. The consequence is that for those themes impact is arithmetically 3× volume, they occupy the top of every impact ranking on this site, and a high-volume theme with honestly-measured low severity — filters returning the wrong providers, at 34 reports — sorts below them. The † marker shows how many of a theme's blocking reports are inferred rather than evidenced.
Coverage 18% of actionable tickets match no theme, and 37% of all intake is excluded as non-actionable before themes are applied. Diagnostics (app version, OS, device) exist on only 47% of tickets, so every platform and version split is a sample, not a census.
Window 2025-12-01 to 2026-09-30, enforced at aggregation rather than trusted from the fetch. Reports are cumulative: theme totals cover the whole window, and per-month figures live on the month pages.
Provenance Generated on a maintainer's machine, not in CI — ticket text never leaves that machine. Only these aggregates are published. A local audit file maps every number here back to the exact ticket keys behind it; it is deliberately not published, for the reason in the note below.
PHI note. AFH tickets contain patient free text and device, session and tracking identifiers. One ticket is one patient, so ticket keys are treated as patient-level data and are never published on this page. Drill-down happens in Jira, which is permission-gated: all tickets in window · still open.

History: every published month — each one frozen as it read the day it went out.
