Patient app feedback · December 2025

Wrong in-network badges cost 2 patients a visit or money; the rest are one-offs.

We read all 28 December tickets: 15 were real reports and 13 were noise (internal tests, survey replies, blank submissions). Five patients said they lost a visit, trip or money. Two of those five trace to the same problem: a provider badged in-network for their plan turned out not to take it. Two more were turned away at the practice for a booked time, each a different cause, and one could not book because the Book button stayed greyed out with no hint why, which has since been fixed. No other problem reached the bar of 3 tickets this month.
