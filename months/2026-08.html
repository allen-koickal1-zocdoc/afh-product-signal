Patient app feedback · August 2026

Wrong in-network listings reached 4 tickets, the ninth month in a row with reports.

We read all 59 August tickets: 33 were real reports and 26 were noise, mostly survey emails, blank submissions and messages with no product problem. Two patients said they lost a visit, trip or money, against 3 on the dashboard: one was billed out of network after booking a provider listed as in-network, and one arrived to find the office had no record of the booking. Wrong in-network listings rose from 3 read tickets in July to 4 and remain the only problem with stated harm that reached the bar. Requests to turn off the assistant-style search came back to the bar at 3 tickets, while complaints about the quality of its results fell from 4 to 1. The dashboard's theme labels matched reading on only 41 of 59 tickets this month, so its theme counts, especially for notifications, should be read as rough.
