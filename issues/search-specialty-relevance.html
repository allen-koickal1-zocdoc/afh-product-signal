Specialty search returns providers from other specialties

Confidence: HighStatus: Quiet since Aug 26Trend: Chronic

Patient searches for a specific specialty or visit reason. Results are padded or led by family medicine, primary care, urgent care, nurse practitioners, or unrelated specialties. Some patients book the top result and only find out at the visit that the provider doesn't practise that specialty, so the visit is wasted. Patients expect only matching specialists, or an explicit 'none found nearby' instead of substitutes.
