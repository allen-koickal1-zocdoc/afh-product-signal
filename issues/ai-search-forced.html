Patients can't find how to turn AI search off

Confidence: HighStatus: OpenTrend: Rising

Since the AI-powered search rolled out (first reports May 2026, rising June and August), patients say the assistant is the only way in. They can't skip it, can't find a toggle to turn it off, and the chat panel takes up the screen. Long-time users ask for the classic search, filters and map back, and some say they'll stop using the app. Privacy and environmental objections also come up.
