Known issues · Insurance (provider network data)

Provider badged in-network for the patient's plan turned out not to be

Confidence: HighStatus: OpenTrend: Chronic

Patient enters their plan, searches, picks a provider badged in-network and books. The office then calls or the claim comes back to say the provider does not accept that plan (or that specific plan variant). Many patients only learn this at the visit or when a bill arrives, so they lose the visit, pay out of pocket, or rebook. Several say it happened on multiple bookings. A recurring sub-pattern is providers who book through a third-party practice network listed as accepting plans they don't. Another is specific plan variants (state/public plans) being lumped into a broader carrier entry.
